# FaceLooker Icon Design
# 512x512 PNG - Clean, modern face tracking icon

## Design Concept:
- Simple face outline with tracking dots
- Clean, minimalist design
- Blue color scheme matching app theme (#6366f1)
- Professional and approachable

## Elements:
1. Central face silhouette (simple outline)
2. Tracking grid dots around the face
3. Clean typography "FL" or face icon
4. Subtle shadow/depth effect

## Colors:
- Primary: #6366f1 (Figma blue)
- Background: White/transparent
- Accent: #f0f0f0 (light gray)

## Usage:
- App icon for PWA
- Favicon for browsers
- Taskbar/desktop icons
